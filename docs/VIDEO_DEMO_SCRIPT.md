# Utsav Demo Video Script

This is a judge-ready, under-4-minute video plan for the Utsav project. It follows the submission checklist and keeps the focus on live testing, clear walkthrough, and AI in action.

## Video goal
Show that Utsav turns a real event brief into a workable, explainable plan using:
- a rules-first planner
- live budget and readiness calculations
- vendor and timeline guidance
- optional GenAI ideas and a conversational assistant

## Recommended recording setup
- Use a 1440x900 browser window in Chrome or Edge
- Keep browser zoom at 100%
- Use OBS, Loom, or Zoom screen recording
- Record with a clear mouse pointer and short pauses on important UI states
- Aim for 3 minutes 15 seconds to 3 minutes 45 seconds total

## Storyboard and narration

### Scene 1 — Landing page and value proposition (0:00–0:25)
Visuals:
- Open the app on the landing page
- Highlight the heading: “Plan your day”
- Show the top navigation and the CTA button

Narration:
“Utsav is an AI-powered event planning assistant for weddings, birthdays, and community events. It helps users turn a rough event idea into a realistic budget, timeline, vendor checklist, and readiness plan.”

On-screen text:
- “Rules first, AI second”
- “Budget • Timeline • Vendors • AI ideas”

### Scene 2 — Live data entry and onboarding (0:25–1:10)
Visuals:
- Click “Start planning” or “Try a sample plan”
- Fill the form live in front of the camera
- Pause on each input so the numbers are visible

Use these exact values:
- Event type: Wedding
- Sub-type: Bengali
- Budget: ₹15,00,000
- Guests: 250
- Venue style: Indoor Hall
- City tier: Tier 1
- Dietary preference: Vegetarian
- Event date: 2027-02-26
- Top priority: Catering

Narration:
“I start with a realistic wedding brief: a Bengali wedding for 250 guests, a ₹15 lakh budget, and indoor venue planning. The planner uses these inputs to generate a full plan in real time.”

Important note:
- Do not show a pre-filled screen
- Type the values live
- Keep each field visible for 1–2 seconds

### Scene 3 — Budget and readiness dashboard (1:10–1:55)
Visuals:
- Show the generated budget section
- Highlight the total budget and per-category allocations
- Show readiness score and next tasks

Narration:
“The planner computes a budget allocation, flags readiness, and identifies the next critical tasks. It is explainable: every number is tied to a real rule, not a hidden black box.”

Callouts to show:
- Budget total
- Readiness score
- Next 5 tasks
- Budget health card

### Scene 4 — Rules and explainability (1:55–2:20)
Visuals:
- Switch to the “Why this plan?” or rule explanation area if available
- Highlight plain-language reasons

Narration:
“This is a rules-first planner. It explains why decisions are made, such as venue, timing, or contingency, so users understand the logic behind the plan.”

On-screen text:
- “Explainable planning”
- “No magic — just transparent logic”

### Scene 5 — GenAI ideas in action (2:20–2:55)
Visuals:
- Scroll to “Personalised ideas”
- Enter a focus prompt such as: “low-waste decor, elder-friendly seating”
- Click “Generate ideas”
- Show the generated list of ideas

Narration:
“Now the AI layer kicks in. It creates context-aware ideas for decor, menu, and planning based on the user’s event, budget, and preferences. The response changes dynamically with the input.”

Important note:
- Show the prompt entering the field
- Show the output appearing
- Say clearly: “This is GenAI in action”

### Scene 6 — AI assistant and real-world help (2:55–3:30)
Visuals:
- Open “Ask Utsav”
- Enter a question such as “Where am I overspending?” or “What should I do next?”
- Show the assistant response

Narration:
“The assistant answers planning questions using the live plan context. It helps with budget concerns, next tasks, and risk-aware recommendations without changing the event plan without confirmation.”

On-screen text:
- “AI uses the current plan context”
- “User confirmation before any change”

### Scene 7 — Closing summary and final CTA (3:30–3:45)
Visuals:
- Show the combined workflow: planner → budget → AI ideas → assistant → export-ready plan
- End on the home screen or final dashboard state

Narration:
“Utsav combines smart planning, explainable numbers, and optional AI to make event planning easier for real people. It works fast, works without AI when needed, and keeps the human in control.”

Final on-screen text:
- “Built for weddings, birthdays, and community events”
- “Rules first, AI second”
- “Public demo link in submission form”

## Exact demo checklist against the submission criteria

### Required elements
- [x] Clear walkthrough of the main app flow
- [x] Live inputs and real-time output
- [x] AI clearly called out and shown in action
- [x] Dynamic AI response, not static fixed content
- [x] Step-by-step pacing with visible actions
- [x] Under 4 minutes total
- [x] Public Google Drive or YouTube submission link

### Avoid these mistakes
- Do not show a pre-filled form without typing inputs
- Do not spend too long on the landing page
- Do not narrate in a rushed or unclear way
- Do not skip the AI section
- Do not leave the link private or gated

## Submission-ready record plan
1. Record the full screen of the app in a Chrome browser window.
2. Keep the entire recording between 3:15 and 3:45.
3. Upload the video to Google Drive or YouTube.
4. Set the link to public or unlisted.
5. Open the link in an incognito/private window to confirm it plays without login.
